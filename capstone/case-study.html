AVEOM OPS Case study · 100xEngineers C7 Capstone

Crew hours and petty cash, recorded where the work happens

AVEOM runs events across the UAE with part-time site crew. Their hours used to reach the office through WhatsApp messages and site sheets, and petty cash through cash in hand and bills in pockets. AVEOM OPS replaces both with one phone app: crew log their own shifts (even with no signal), petty cash holders confirm the money they receive and photograph their bills, and an AI reads each bill so they only check and submit.

How it works

Two flows, one app

Shift hours

  1. Crew
    Opens the app on site and logs start, end and project. No signal needed.
  2. The app saves it on the phone and sends it as soon as there's a connection.
  3. The server checks the hours and wage are calculated correctly, and flags overlaps or very long shifts.
  4. Production Manager
    Reviews the fortnight, corrects anything wrong (every edit is logged), downloads the timesheet.

Petty cash

  1. Owner
    Records a transfer or cash handover, with optional proof.
  2. Recipient
    Confirms receipt, or reports a problem with a note.
  3. Photographs each bill. The AI reads it and fills the form; they check and submit.
  4. Production Manager
    Approves or rejects each bill. Balances and statements update straight away.
Benefits

Who gains what

Crew and petty cash holders

  • Log hours while they're fresh, not from memory days later.
  • Works without signal on site; nothing is lost.
  • See their own fortnight: hours, earnings, balance, and every bill's status.
  • Photo in, form filled — no typing totals off crumpled receipts.
  • Proof they were paid and proof they handed in bills, with times.

Production Manager

  • No more chasing WhatsApp threads and site managers for timesheets.
  • Errors caught at entry: overlaps, long shifts, old receipts, foreign currency.
  • One place to correct a shift or reject a bill, with the reason recorded.
  • Full control of access: create logins, switch shifts or petty cash on per person, deactivate instantly.
  • Private logs of every sign-in, confirmation and change, to find when a mistake happened.

Owner and Accountant

  • Live view of how much petty cash each person holds.
  • No “I never got it”: every payment is confirmed or disputed in the app.
  • Payroll-ready Excel by person and project, totals that tie out.
  • Pay-period views that match the 1st–15th and 16th–end cycle.
  • Bills arrive checked, with the original photo one tap away.
Security and data integrity

Built so records can be trusted

Who can do what

  • Roles enforced on the server, not just hidden buttons. 88 automated checks try every forbidden action as every role.
  • Separation of duties: only the Owner sends money; only the Production Manager approves bills; nobody approves their own.
  • Logins created by the Production Manager — no self sign-up. Deactivating someone signs them out everywhere.

Records can't be quietly changed

  • Crew can't edit a shift once it's sent, or claim more hours or pay than the times allow — the server re-checks the maths.
  • Payments, approvals and confirmations are written only by the server.
  • Bill photos can't be replaced or deleted after upload.
  • Every correction keeps the before and after, with who and when.

Data protection

  • Hosted on Google Firebase, data stored in Mumbai (asia-south1).
  • Recovery: any minute of the last 7 days can be restored, plus daily backups kept 14 days.
  • The AI key is kept in Google Secret Manager, readable only by the bill-reading function — never on phones or in the code.
  • Passwords handled by Firebase Authentication; failed sign-ins are slowed down and logged.
Ease of use

Made for a phone on a busy site

  • No app store: open a link and “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Username and password — no email needed for crew.
  • Each person sees only what they use: shifts, petty cash, or both.
  • A shift is four fields; hours and pay appear as you type.
  • A bill is one photo and a check of four filled fields.
  • “Action needed” cards for payments waiting to be confirmed.
  • Big tap targets, light and dark mode, readable in sunlight.
  • Updates arrive by themselves — one tap on “Update now”.
  • Clear status: synced, waiting for signal, pending review.
What the evidence changed

Three things real use and testing taught us

Each of these came from testing or from the first days of live use, and each changed the design.

Found

The AI invented a year

A bank-app screenshot showed “16 September” with no year. The AI filled in 2023. It read the day right; the person then changed the day to the 9th, which was wrong. The app's “1,126 days old” warning caught it at review.

Changed

The AI now says when no year is printed

The server then uses the most recent past date. Each bill also records which AI fields the person edited, so we can measure where the AI and the person each go wrong.

Found

Bills seemed to vanish

Bills were filed under the receipt date, so an old receipt landed in a fortnight from years ago and never showed on the current statement.

Changed

Bills count when submitted

Statements and reports now use the submission date and show the receipt date beside it. Receipts over 60 days old are flagged.

Found

Shifts needing rounding were rejected

Automated testing showed the server's check refused any shift that needed rounding up (9 h 40 → 10 h), before any crew hit it.

Changed

The check was rewritten and pinned by tests

Sixteen rounding cases now run with every change, including the ones that must be refused.
