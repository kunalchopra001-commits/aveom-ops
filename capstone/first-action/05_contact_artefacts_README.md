# 05 — Contact artefacts

**One screenshot per user. No notes count without this.**

- `05_contact_user1.png`
- `05_contact_user2.png`

## Must be visible in the shot

- [ ] The message you sent, in your own words
- [ ] Their reply
- [ ] **A timestamp** — this is the tie-break criterion
- [ ] Who it is (name or number; blur or crop anything sensitive)

Screenshot the moment you press send, before they reply. Then screenshot again once
they answer. Keep both.
